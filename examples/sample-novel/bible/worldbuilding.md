# Worldbuilding

## The Blackout
A global electromagnetic event, seven years before the story begins. It
extinguished all digital storage — hard drives, solid-state, even magnetic tape
above a certain density. Paper and oral record survived. The exact cause is
unknown and, for this volume, irrelevant: the world remembers only the effect.

## The Lighthouse
One of the last functioning analog structures on the coast. Its beam is the only
navigational aid for hundreds of kilometers; if it goes dark, ships are lost.
This is why the keeper cannot simply leave.

## The Archive
Aren maintains a paper archive in the lighthouse basement — charts, tide tables,
fragments of pre-Blackout maps. The brass key he carries opens it.

## Grey Harbor
A mainland settlement, spoken of but not yet seen. Mira reportedly lives there.
Its existence and nature are unconfirmed from Aren's point of view.
