# Anti-AI Rules

Phrases and patterns to reject in generated prose. A style reviewer flags any
occurrence.

## Banned phrases
- "couldn't help but"
- "couldn't help but notice"
- "a testament to"
- "a symphony of"
- "a tapestry of"
- "in the realm of"
- "it was nothing short of"
- "seemed to almost"
- "a stark reminder"
- "whispered the secrets of"

## Banned patterns
- Triplet rhythm ("X, Y, and Z") more than once per 500 words.
- Rhetorical questions in narration.
- Em-dash-paired mirrors more than twice per page ("the cold — and the heat
  beneath it").
- Opening a sentence with "It was" more than once per chapter.
- Adverb + verb pairings where the adverb adds nothing ("softly whispered",
  "loudly shouted").
- Explaining an emotion after an image, gesture, object, or line of dialogue has
  already made it legible.
- Generic body shorthand (caught breath, pounding heart, widened eyes, clenched
  jaw) unless the physical fact changes the scene.
- Ending a scene with a generalized statement of what the moment means.

## Revision priorities
- Prefer the watch, key, note, lamp, weather, and work of keeping the light over
  abstract emotional labels.
- Vary sentence structure for pressure, landscape, memory, and dialogue instead
  of repeating a generated template.
- Benchmarks establish craft constraints only; never copy an author's
  distinctive phrase.
