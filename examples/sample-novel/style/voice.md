# Voice

Spare. Concrete. Short sentences under emotional pressure, longer sentences for
landscape and memory. Present-tense immediacy in scene openings, settling into
past tense. Minimal dialogue tags — let the dialogue carry itself. No
adjectives where a noun will do.

**Avoid:** "suddenly," "seemed to," "couldn't help but," rhetorical questions in
prose, triplet rhythms ("X, Y, and Z" more than once per page).

**Favor:** physical objects as emotional anchors (the watch, the key, the note),
the sea as a clock, silence as speech.
