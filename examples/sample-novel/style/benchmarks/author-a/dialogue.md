# Dialogue benchmark

"You kept it lit."

Aren set the cup beside the stove. The tin clicked against iron.

"Ships still come."

"That wasn't what I asked."

He folded the tide chart along its oldest crease. Rain worked at the window. The woman waited, and the room filled with everything he had decided not to say.

## Craft notes

- Dialogue tags disappear when speaker order and physical action make attribution clear.
- Objects interrupt speech and hold emotional weight.
- Subtext remains unstated; the last sentence names pressure without explaining its cause.
