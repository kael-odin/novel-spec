# Psychology benchmark

At seven past three the watch became a useless thing. Aren wound it every winter anyway. One turn of the crown. Two. Then the small resistance of a spring that remembered its work and had nowhere to send it.

He did not call this hope. Hope asked for a future. This was maintenance.

## Craft notes

- Emotion is anchored in a repeated physical task.
- Short sentences carry the interpretive turn.
- The passage names an emotion only to reject the easy label and replace it with a concrete one.
