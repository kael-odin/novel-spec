# Scene description sample — author-a

A reference sample for how landscape/scene-setting prose should read in this
voice. Extracted from a deconstruction of the target author's work.

---

The dock ended where the dock always ended, thirty paces past the shed, and the
water beyond it was the grey of old iron. No wind. The gulls stood on the posts
like they were waiting for something, and they were: they were waiting for the
fish-guts the afternoon boats would bring, the way everything on the water waits
for the thing that is coming. The light was low and yellow and it did not reach
far.
